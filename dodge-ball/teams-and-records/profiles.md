# Dodge-ball Top Eight Profiles

## 1. Detroit Detonators

Detroit plays a relentless, direct style built around quick center-field wins
and coordinated goal-line pressure.

- **2024:** Reached the inaugural final after leading the league in goals.
- **2025:** Won the championship with a league-best defensive record.
- **2026:** Returned as the top-ranked team after an undefeated regular season.

## 2. Charlotte Chargers

Charlotte relies on speed, wide-field spacing, and rapid transitions from a
defensive clearance to a scoring chance.

- **2024:** Finished sixth and earned the league's breakthrough-team award.
- **2025:** Advanced to the semifinals on the strength of its fast-break game.
- **2026:** Claimed the second ranking after reaching the conference final.

## 3. Chicago Chicanes

Chicago specializes in tight formations and sharp changes of direction that
make its ball movement difficult to read.

- **2024:** Made the playoffs in the final qualifying spot.
- **2025:** Finished as championship runner-up.
- **2026:** Earned third place through disciplined, low-foul play.

## 4. Phoenix Powerplays

Phoenix uses patient possession and powerful long clearances to stretch the
expanded field before attacking through open lanes.

- **2024:** Missed the playoffs by one place.
- **2025:** Reached the quarterfinals in its first postseason appearance.
- **2026:** Rose to fourth after winning its division.

## 5. Cleveland Crankshafts

Cleveland is a physical but controlled team that excels at contesting the ball
in traffic and turning rebounds into immediate pressure.

- **2024:** Finished fourth in the regular-season table.
- **2025:** Fell in the semifinals after a close extra-time match.
- **2026:** Holds fifth after another deep playoff run.

## 6. Austin Afterburners

Austin plays with high tempo, sending multiple cars forward and trusting its
rotations to recover quickly when possession changes.

- **2024:** Debuted with a ninth-place finish.
- **2025:** Made the playoffs and won its first postseason match.
- **2026:** Reached the top eight after its highest-scoring regular season.

## 7. Seattle Slipstreams

Seattle's strength is drafting-style teamwork: cars create lanes for one
another, then strike the ball from unexpected angles.

- **2024:** Finished as a competitive expansion side outside the playoff line.
- **2025:** Qualified for the quarterfinals.
- **2026:** Earned seventh after a strong late-season surge.

## 8. Baltimore Boltcutters

Baltimore combines compact defending with calculated counterattacks and has a
reputation for closing tight matches.

- **2024:** Recorded the league's fewest goals conceded.
- **2025:** Reached the semifinals and finished third overall.
- **2026:** Secured the final top-eight spot with a division-playoff win.
