# Dodge-ball Teams and Records

This folder tracks Dodge-ball teams, rosters, results, standings, and records.

Each club registers eight driver-and-vehicle pairings. Five vehicles take the
field for a match, while three substitutes may enter at approved stoppages.
Any Dodge car or Dodge-badged truck from any year is eligible after inspection.

See [team dossiers](teams.md) for colors, fictional sponsors, leadership, and
the full 64-driver grid.
