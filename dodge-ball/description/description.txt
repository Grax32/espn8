# Dodge-ball

Not dodgeball. It has a dash.

Dodge-ball is a five-versus-five vehicle sport played with Dodge cars and
Dodge-badged trucks on an
expanded soccer field. Teams use controlled contact with a 10-foot regulation
ball to drive it into an opposing goal that spans the full width of the field.
There is no goalie: every car attacks, defends, and creates space.

The game follows the broad rhythm of soccer—kickoff, open play, goals, halves,
and stoppage time—but replaces footwork with vehicle positioning and ball
control. Any Dodge car or Dodge-badged truck from any year may compete when it
meets event safety and inspection requirements.

The sport rewards pace, turning skill, coordinated formations, and smart use of
the expanded field. Teams must keep the giant ball moving while preventing a
clear lane to their own full-width goal.

See the rule summary and detailed rules in the `rules` folder.
