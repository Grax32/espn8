# Dodge-ball Final Eight Hero Image Plan

## Purpose

Create one editorial hero image for each tournament game. Each image should
show the competing teams fighting for the win on a Dodge-ball field and leave
clear room for a game headline on the page.

## Shared art direction

- **Field:** all packed dirt, with tire ruts, dust, and a faint painted end
  line at each end.
- **Ball:** include one clearly visible regulation Dodge-ball in every image.
  It should be active in the play—flying between the teams, skidding through
  the dirt, or immediately contested—rather than appearing as incidental
  background detail.
- **Goal treatment:** never show a goal, net, posts, or other physical goal
  structure. The end line is the goal.
- **Teams:** show at least one vehicle from each team; two or three vehicles
  per side is preferred where the composition permits.
- **Vehicle livery:** use the team colors and place that team's icon-plus-name
  logo prominently on each car door.
- **Framing:** low, wide motorsport-editorial camera angle; strong action,
  dust, and clear separation between the two teams. Preserve open negative
  space in the upper-left or upper-right for headline copy.
- **Consistency:** use the same late-afternoon light, dirt texture, and
  high-energy photographic treatment across the seven images.
- **Avoid:** grass, turf, stadium scenery, cones, netting, physical goals,
  real vehicle-brand marks, unrelated logos, watermark text, or a visible
  crowd as the primary subject.

## Suggested work order

1. Generate and approve the Friday championship hero as the visual benchmark.
2. Generate each earlier-round hero using the same camera, field, and lighting
   language.
3. Review every image for team-color accuracy, logo visibility, a clearly
   visible active Dodge-ball, and the no-physical-goal rule before placing it
   on the site.
