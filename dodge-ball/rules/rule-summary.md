# Dodge-ball Rule Summary

## The game

- Two teams of five Dodge vehicles compete on an expanded soccer field.
- The regulation ball is 10 feet in diameter.
- Goals span the full width of the field; there is no goalie.
- A goal counts when the entire ball crosses the goal line.
- The team with more goals at the end of regulation wins.

## Match format

- Matches have two 20-minute halves and a 10-minute halftime.
- Play begins each half and resumes after every goal with a center-field kickoff.
- The clock stops for injuries, inspections, and referee-directed stoppages.
- A tied elimination match goes to two 10-minute extra-time periods, then a
  best-of-five ball-off if still tied.

## Play and fouls

- Cars may use controlled, forward-motion contact to move the ball.
- A car may not ram, sideswipe, pin, or strike another car away from the ball.
- No car may enter the goal safety zone except while following through on a
  legal play of the ball.
- Major fouls result in a free drive; dangerous play can bring a yellow or red
  card.

## Teams and equipment

- Any Dodge car or Dodge-badged truck from any year is eligible after technical inspection.
- Each team has five active cars and may make substitutions at approved
  stoppages.
- Drivers must follow event safety requirements and referee instructions.
