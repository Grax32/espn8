# Eccentric Sports News Site Style Guide

## Brand

**Name:** Eccentric Sports News

Eccentric Sports News covers unusual, imaginative, and high-energy sports with
the urgency and clarity of a major sports network.

## Logo

Use a lightning-inspired mark: a bold, angular lightning bolt paired with the
**Eccentric Sports News** wordmark. The bolt should feel fast, electric, and
slightly unconventional. Keep it simple enough to read at small sizes.

- Preferred lockup: bolt to the left of the wordmark.
- Use the bolt alone for favicons, social avatars, and compact badges.
- Preserve clear space around the logo equal to the bolt's widest section.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or add effects that reduce legibility.

## Color

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electric yellow | Primary brand accent | `#FFD400` |
| Deep navy | Main background and headlines | `#071A33` |
| White | Primary text and open space | `#FFFFFF` |
| Bolt blue | Secondary accent and links | `#00AEEF` |
| Signal red | Scores, alerts, and breaking coverage | `#E53935` |

Use electric yellow for the lightning bolt and key calls to action. Use deep
navy as the primary background so the bolt has strong contrast.

## Typography

- Headlines: bold, condensed sans serif with a sports-broadcast feel.
- Body: clean, highly readable sans serif.
- Scores and statistics: tabular numerals when available.

Keep headlines direct, energetic, and specific. Use sentence case for body copy
and title case for major section headings.

## Layout and Voice

Use a clear editorial grid with prominent event coverage, scores, team records,
and strong imagery. Lead stories with the unusual detail that makes the sport
memorable, then explain rules and results plainly.

Write with enthusiasm and precision. Treat every sport as real competition;
avoid mocking language.

## Dodge-ball Event Coverage

For the special Dodge-ball full-coverage event, give live scores and team
records prominent placement. Use the electric yellow bolt as the event marker
alongside coverage of the 10-foot regulation ball, five-car teams, and
full-field-width goals.
